## Welcome to Singapore

Arrive at [Changi Airport](poi:changi-airport) around 11:10. Transfer to [Robertson Quay](poi:robertson-quay).

### Afternoon
- Check into serviced apartment
- Settle in, freshen up after the overnight flight
- Explore [Robertson Quay](poi:robertson-quay) neighbourhood on foot

### Evening
- Walk along the Singapore River pedestrian paths
- Dinner at one of the riverside restaurants
- Early night to recover from jet lag
